## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard visualizes Gold-layer KPIs generated from the flash-sale streaming
pipeline, giving a business-facing view of demand and conversion behavior during
high-traffic sale events.

**What it shows:**
- **Conversion funnel** — views → cart → purchase, highlighting drop-off points
- **Cart abandonment rate** — percentage of carts started but not completed
- **Average order value (AOV)** — revenue per completed purchase
- **Demand pressure index** — event volume per unit sold, used to flag high-demand products in real time
- **Product & category trends** — which items and categories drove the most activity during the simulated flash sale

*Note: all data is synthetically generated to simulate flash-sale traffic patterns — see the "Synthetic Data" note above for realistic benchmark context.*
